The road to the exam

Season plan

Three phases. Reading and vocabulary come first — that's the biggest gap, and stronger vocabulary lifts listening and writing at the same time.

Phase 1 · Pre-season

Rebuild the base

Sept – Dec 2026
  • Learn the shape of each paper (parts, question types, timing).
  • Daily vocabulary: build the word bank, review little and often.
  • Reading: skim first, then underline only the key information.
  • Listening: predict before you hear; learn signpost words.
  • One part at a time — accuracy before speed.

Phase 2 · Mid-season

Build accuracy

Jan – Mar 2027
  • Full papers, relaxed timing, then review every wrong answer.
  • Listening at real speed: spot the traps, don't match word-for-word.
  • Writing: richer vocabulary, natural expressions, ~100 words.
  • Start Speaking: Parts 1–4 with a partner.
  • Fill the Post-Match log after each test.

Sharpen for the whistle

  • Full timed mocks under exam conditions.

  • Polish Speaking and Writing to sound natural and detailed.

The tactics

Playbook

Quick reminders for each paper — the moves that make the difference. Open one before you practise that skill.

📖 Reading · 6 parts · 45 min›
  • Read the instructions and the question first, then the text.
  • Skim once for the general idea before hunting for answers.
  • Underline only the key information — names, numbers, opinion words.
  • If a word is hard, read the whole sentence — the meaning is often around it.
  • Parts 5 & 6 test grammar and set phrases: think what word fits, not just what makes sense.
  • Never leave a blank — a sensible guess can be right.
🎧 Listening · 4 parts · ~30 min›
  • Use the pause to predict: a number? a place? a time?
  • The answer is usually in different words from the question — don't wait for the exact match.
  • Watch for signposts: but, however, actually, in the end.
  • Miss one? Let it go and catch the next — don't freeze.
  • You hear it twice: first for the answer, second to check.
  • Spell gap-fill answers carefully — practise the words you keep meeting.
✍️ Writing · 2 tasks · 45 min›
  • Answer every point in the question — marked first.
  • Plan for one minute before you write.
  • Aim for ~100 words — room to show detailed, natural language.
  • Vary language: linkers (although, so that, as well as), tenses, less common words.
  • Match the tone to the task (friendly email vs. article).
  • Leave two minutes to check spelling, verbs, punctuation.
🗣️ Speaking · 4 parts · with a partner›
  • Give reasons and examples — never just "yes"/"no".
  • Word won't come? Describe it: "the thing you use to…". Keep going.
  • Photo task: describe what you see and what is happening.
  • React to your partner — it's a conversation, not a speech.
  • Speak clearly and steadily; a small pause is fine.
