The targets

On every practice paper, aim past the pass zone and towards flying colours. The dashed line is the June mock — where the season starts. Log a new test and the bars move.

The road to the exam

Season plan

Three phases. Reading and vocabulary come first — that's the biggest gap, and stronger vocabulary lifts listening and writing at the same time.

Phase 1 · Pre-season

Rebuild the base

Sept – Dec 2026
  • Learn the shape of each paper (parts, question types, timing).
  • Daily vocabulary: build the word bank, review little and often.
  • Reading: skim first, then underline only the key information.
  • Listening: predict before you hear; learn signpost words.
  • One part at a time — accuracy before speed.
Checkpoint mock · mid-DecReading ≥ 12/32 · Listening ≥ 13/25 · Writing ≥ 27/40
🎯 Hit two of three → you pick the next film in English.
Phase 2 · Mid-season

Build accuracy

Jan – Mar 2027
  • Full papers, relaxed timing, then review every wrong answer.
  • Listening at real speed: spot the traps, don't match word-for-word.
  • Writing: richer vocabulary, natural expressions, ~100 words.
  • Start Speaking: Parts 1–4 with a partner.
  • Fill the Post-Match log after each test.
Checkpoint mock · late MarReading ≥ 18/32 · Listening ≥ 16/25 · Writing ≥ 31/40
🎯 Reach the pass zone in all three → celebration of your choice.
Phase 3 · Finals

Sharpen for the whistle

Apr – exam 2027
  • Full timed mocks under exam conditions.
  • Re-read the error log — close the repeating mistakes.
  • Polish Speaking and Writing to sound natural and detailed.
  • Light, steady revision — arrive rested and confident.
Flying-colours targetReading ≥ 24/32 · Listening ≥ 19/25 · Writing ≥ 32/40
🎯 Whistle blows — whatever the score, you turned up ready.
